JDG Productions

What is JDG Productions?

JDG Productions is the home of Joe Green's shows and software — audio made to be heard, and technology built to be used by everyone. Every production under this name is created with accessibility at its center: if it carries the JDG Productions name, it was made for all ears and all users.
